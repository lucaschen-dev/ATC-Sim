# ATC Sim v1.1.6 · Build 56

- Corrected selected runway, apron, and stand guidance around the reported locations at KLAX, VHHH, and RCTP. KLAX now distinguishes infield grass and the Terminal 5 construction area from pavement. Los Angeles entry curves blend into the runway centerline and continue along it for 7 metres. Marked transverse crossings remain continuous. Hong Kong retains near-center ends of its charted taxiway curves. At Macao, G now merges into the runway centerline, and the gap in the C3 connector is closed.
- Stand identifiers use tinted boxes beside their guide endpoints and avoid aircraft symbols and terminal roofs. A verified secondary stand marking is hidden while its identifier remains available.
- At close zoom, ground guidance stays vector-rendered and fine stand lines have more contrast.
- Ground aircraft take up less space in the airport overview and stop growing beyond a screen-size limit in close views. Aircraft still retain their relative length and wingspan.
- Scrolling an open radar procedure list now moves the list while the radar keeps its zoom. Radar scrolling resumes when the list closes.

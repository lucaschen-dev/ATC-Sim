# Build 25 — Map annotations without leader lines

**Build:** 25  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Record:** Retrospective release notes

## Changes

- Removed annotation leaders from waypoint names, runway end numbers, nearby-airport labels and restricted/danger-area names.
- Kept map text clear of selected-flight and sector route overlays.
- Improved contour contrast and preserved published airspace identifiers.
- Used 100-unit altitude steps and 10-kt IAS steps with procedure-limit validation.

## Scope and limitations

Operational flight paths, localizer geometry and aircraft tag leaders retain their separate meanings.

# Build 21 — Hong Kong, Taoyuan, Pudong and Urumqi

**Build:** 21  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Record:** Retrospective release notes

## Changes

- Added VHHH, RCTP, ZSPD and ZWWW, bringing the bundled library to 19 airports.
- Integrated individual operational stand pools, airport procedures, elevation grids and airspace data.
- Used feet for Hong Kong and Taoyuan and retained Hong Kong's source-defined ILS arming gate.
- Kept Urumqi's chart-closed runway as reference-only.

## Scope and limitations

Operational stand pools are reviewed subsets, not every printed stand number. Unsupported procedure legs remain reference-only.

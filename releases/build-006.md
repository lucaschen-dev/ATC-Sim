# Build 6 — ATC Sim identity and multi-airport foundation

**Build:** 6  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Record:** Retrospective release notes

## Changes

- Renamed the product ATC Sim and established reusable airport packages.
- Expanded chart-backed ground and air operations beyond Guangzhou, including Lhasa, Hongqiao, Beijing Capital and Chengdu Shuangliu.
- Introduced high-elevation airport handling and retained separate per-airport progress.

# ATC Sim v1.1.6 · Build 55

## Ground

- Reworked parking-bay guidance at Porto Vela (VMMC) and added close-zoom stand guidance at Meridian Isles (VHHH). Porto Vela's terminal-side bays follow their charted axes, and its cargo leads are drawn from the parking diagram. Major runway connectors are visible.
- Checked stand markings and positions across all 20 bundled airports. Corrected the position of stand 511 at Akari Coast (RCTP), restored missing stop-end markings for verified stands at Nordhaven (ZBAA) and Aster Bay (ZGGG), and decluttered overlapping parking variants at Pacific Bluffs.
- Pushback uses the same path for the aircraft, green clearance line, and conflict forecast. Paved turns blend into the straight segments at eligible stand exits.
- Active aircraft are easier to pick out at common zoom levels. Size still varies by airframe.

## Pacific Bluffs (KLAX)

- Added Ground, Approach, and Area sessions. Four runways support eight directional operations, with modeled runway markings, terrain, and FAA procedure data.
- The airfield is based on the FAA airport diagram, the LAWA movement-area map, and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Some taxiway pavement outlines are reconstructed from mapped centerlines.

## Radio and settings

- The Chinese voice list prefers Mandarin-compatible voices. The installed build keeps the same Keychain entry for the player's API key.
- Radio transmit and release sounds are shorter and quieter. Settings opens inside the game window and the green crosshair spans the full window while it is open.

See the [player guide](../docs/HOW_TO_PLAY.md) for controls and clearance workflows.
